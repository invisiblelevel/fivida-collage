What's inside

Everything you need to make quick, good-looking collages.

🎨

34 Layouts

15 regular grids and ~19 irregular layouts — Journal, Mosaic, Film strip, Polaroid, Pyramid and more.

🔷

7 Figures

Circle, Ellipse, Pyramid, Hexagon, Honeycomb, Diamonds, Stars. Each with adjustable size and count.

🔄

Free Rotation

Grab the handle and turn any photo to any angle. Auto-scaling keeps corners filled — no empty gaps.

✋

Drag & Drop

Swap photos between cells directly on the canvas. Replace or remove any photo with a single click.

🖼️

Borders & Shadows

Thickness, color, corner radius, shadow intensity. Custom HSV picker for background and border colors.

💾

Export Anywhere

PNG, JPEG, or WebP. Resolution up to 4096×4096. Everything runs on your machine.

🌍

Three Languages

English, Русский, 中文. Auto-detects your system language on first launch.

🔒

Fully Offline

No clouds, no subscriptions, no data collection. Installer bundles WebView2 — works without internet.

Ready to build your collage?

Windows 10 (1903+) / Windows 11 (64-bit). Free and open source.
